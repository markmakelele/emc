# EMC Framework

## E — Experience

The felt and interpreted condition of a system: perception, comprehension, confidence, friction, memory and interaction.

## M — Mechanics

The operational condition: rules, sequences, constraints, interfaces, infrastructure and implementation.

## N — Necessity

The reason a system persists: purpose, dependency, consequence and the problem it resolves.

## Relationship

```text
Mechanics → Experience → Necessity
       ↘       ↑       ↙
          SYSTEM VALUE
```

The arrows are not intended as a fixed causal law. They are a working model for examining how operational structure, human experience and perceived necessity interact.
