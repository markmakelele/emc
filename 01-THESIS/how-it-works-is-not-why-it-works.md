# How It Works Is Not Why It Works

## The distinction

**How it works** describes the mechanical process, steps, rules, interfaces and conditions that make a system operate.

**Why it works** asks what makes that operation meaningful, trusted, adopted, repeated or necessary.

Knowing the mechanism is therefore not equivalent to understanding the system's deeper purpose or value.

## Working questions

- What makes the system function?
- What makes the experience coherent?
- What makes the system necessary?
- What changes when the system is removed?
- Which factors are mechanical, experiential and necessary?

## EMC

EMC uses three connected lenses:

`Experience · Mechanics · Necessity`

The project studies how these factors reinforce or undermine one another through visual, interaction and information-design experiments.
