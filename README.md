# EMC
## How It Works Is Not Why It Works

**EMC — Experience · Mechanics · Necessity**

A design research system investigating the difference between understanding **how a system operates** and understanding **why that system works, persists, or matters**.

## Thesis

> How it works is not why it works.

Knowing the mechanics of a system explains its operation. It does not necessarily explain why people use it, why they trust it, why they return, why it becomes necessary, or what happens when it is removed.

## EMC model

### Mechanics
The operational layer: processes, rules, interfaces, workflows, roles and infrastructure.

### Experience
The human layer: perception, friction, confidence, cognition, emotion, memory, comprehension and interaction.

### Necessity
The reason the system deserves to exist: the problem it resolves, the dependency it creates, and the consequence of removing it.

## Working proposition

```text
System Value ≈ Mechanics × Experience² × Necessity
```

This is a research model, not a quantitative measurement. It expresses the proposition that functioning mechanics alone do not guarantee meaningful experience or necessity.

## Research → making

```text
RESEARCH
   ↓
QUESTION
   ↓
FRAMEWORK
   ↓
VISUAL STUDY
   ↓
INTERACTION EXPERIMENT
   ↓
PROTOTYPE
   ↓
DOCUMENTATION
   ↓
OUTPUT
```

The repository treats prototypes as evidence of the research rather than as isolated finished designs.

## Repository structure

```text

03-FRAMEWORK/      models and relationships between E/M/N
04-VISUAL-STUDIES/ typography, grids, hierarchy, texture, contrast, poster studies
05-INTERACTION/    hover, glow, overlay, click-pause, sound and modes
06-PROTOTYPES/     iterative HTML studies
07-ASSETS/         images, textures, type and audio

09-OUTPUTS/        web, posters, publications and presentations
```

## Current prototype

`06-PROTOTYPES/v05-script-layout/index.html` is the current editorial/script study. It contains the black-and-white poster translation, Fira Code typography, interactive section states, sound response and a top-left Script / Standard control.
